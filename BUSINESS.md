# Boleia: business description

## One-liner

Boleia is a ride-sharing app for Angola that connects drivers who have empty seats with passengers going the same way.

## Short description (hero section)

Going somewhere? Someone's probably already driving there. Boleia lets verified drivers share the empty seats in their car, and lets passengers book a seat in seconds. It's cheaper than going alone and safer than flagging a ride on the street, and you can travel across the city or across the country.

## Longer description (about section)

Boleia ("a lift" in Portuguese) is a ride-sharing marketplace built for Angola. Drivers already making a trip, whether from Talatona to Luanda Centro or from Luanda to Benguela, Huambo or Lobito, publish the route, the departure time and the seats they have free. Passengers search for a ride, book a seat and pay in the app. Drivers cover their fuel and travel costs, passengers pay a fair, fixed price per seat in kwanzas, and fewer cars on the road means less traffic for everyone.

Safety comes first. Every driver is checked by hand before they can publish a trip: national ID, driving licence, a live selfie and their vehicle documents. During a trip, passengers and drivers have an SOS button that shares their live location with the Boleia operations team, who respond around the clock.

## How it works

### For passengers

1. **Search:** enter where you're going and when.
2. **Book:** choose a ride, see the driver, car, rating and price, and reserve your seat.
3. **Pay securely:** pay by card in the app. If a trip is cancelled, you're refunded automatically.
4. **Travel:** meet your driver and go.

### For drivers

1. **Get verified:** upload your ID, licence, selfie and vehicle documents.
2. **Publish a trip:** set your route, stops, departure time and seats. The app suggests a fair price.
3. **Fill your seats:** passengers book and pay in advance.
4. **Get paid:** your earnings are paid out automatically.

## Key benefits (feature cards)

- **Verified drivers:** every driver's identity, licence and vehicle are reviewed by our team.
- **SOS with live location:** one tap alerts our team, who can see where you are.
- **Fixed, fair prices:** the price per seat is shown before you book, with no haggling.
- **Cashless and secure:** card payment in the app, with automatic refunds when a trip is cancelled.
- **City and long-distance:** daily trips around Luanda and intercity routes across Angola.
- **Travel your way:** see luggage space, stops and preferences (no smoking, music) before booking.
- **Ratings:** passengers and drivers rate each other after every trip.
- **Portuguese and English:** the app and support are available in both.
- **Real support:** help centre, support tickets and reports, handled by a local team.
